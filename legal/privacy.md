## Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

This explains what the BAD App keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. The app is run by The BAD Club LLC, Dubai, UAE (licence No. 2649469.01), called "BAD", "we" or "us" here.

**What we keep**

- **Your account:** your email address, and your password if you don't sign in with Google. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase; we never see them.
- **Your card:** your name, nickname, number, photo, card colour, the sports you play, your level, "About me", your "Currently" status and your membership.
- **Contact details:** mobile, WhatsApp and Instagram, if you add them.
- **Playing and booking:** the sessions you book, check-ins, games and scores, points, BADges, props, and what you owe or have paid. We record how you paid (cash, bank transfer, Ziina or Careem Pay). The app never takes card payments or stores card or bank details.
- **What you post:** Feed posts, photos and reactions.
- **Error reports:** if the app breaks on your phone, it sends the app version, the screen and the error message. Never your name or contact details.

**Why we keep it**

To run the club: approve members, book sessions, organise courts, take payments, award points and BADges, and keep the community safe. We don't sell your data, show ads or share it with advertisers.

**Who sees what**

- **Approved members** see your card, About me, Currently status, departments, points, BADges, props and Feed posts.
- **Admins and crew** also see your bookings, check-ins and payments, to run sessions.
- **Only you and full admins** see your contact details.
- **The public** sees nothing about you. Session pages show only the session.

**Where it's stored**

With Google Firebase (database and sign-in) and a Google Sheet the club uses for points. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may store data outside the UAE.

**How long we keep it**

While your account is open. If you delete it, your card, contact details, posts and photos are removed at once. Payment records are kept for 5 years for the club's accounts, without your contact details.

**Your rights**

You can see and change your card anytime in My BAD. You can ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or delete your account yourself in My BAD (Delete my account, at the bottom). For anything else, email thebadclub.co@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

**Children**

BAD is for players 18 and over. Players aged 16 and 17 can join with a parent's or guardian's permission. We don't knowingly keep data about anyone under 16.

**Changes**

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell members in the app.
