# Badminton Queuing: how a session runs

For crew, Game Masters and admins. Wednesday and Friday sessions. The maths behind every rule is in the Badminton Queuing plan.

## Before the session (admins)
- New badminton sessions start at 2 courts, 2 hours, 16 players and a waiting list of 4.
- Courts → Set up shows the expected games each. Raise the player cap, change the waiting list or extend the time here. Each control shows the effect first.

## At the venue
1. **Open the court board** (Courts → Open the court board).
2. **Check in.** Players scan the QR code, or crew check them in by name. Walk-ins are added by name.
3. **Levels.** On the check-in list, set each player's BAD level (F to A+) and W/M category. Their own pick shows as "says …". Leave UNRATED until you've seen them play. A rating override replaces the table rating; it is never added on top.

## Running games
- **Fill from rotation** puts the next four on a court: fewest games first, late arrivals one game behind, then longest wait. Teams are balanced by level, with fresh partners. Anyone passed over goes next.
- Swap anyone before **Start**. After Start, players can't be moved: Finish or Cancel first.
- The format (To 21 or To 30) is suggested for each game: 21 when the session is full, 30 below it while everyone can still reach 5 games. Tap it to switch before Start.
- **Finish** frees the court, brings on Next up (or the next four) and keeps Next up prepared.
- **Cancel** asks for a reason. No result counts and the players keep their place in line.
- If the 5-game check says people will fall short, extend the time or add a court.

## Scores
1. A player sends the final score from their phone (Your team vs Opponents).
2. A player from the **other** team confirms it, or says something's wrong.
3. Support (badminton admins and the session's Game Master) finalises it on **Scores**. Only final scores count.

On Scores, games are grouped as Ready to finalise, Disputed, Needs confirming and Needs a score.
- **Other team unavailable:** finalise what was sent. The reason is recorded.
- **Guests without a card** can't send scores, so Support enters them.
- **Corrections** to a final score need a reason. Every finalise and correction is logged.

## Standings and BADDEST PLAYER
- Ranking: most wins, then most points (30-point games scaled to 21), then fewer games played. An exact tie shares the title.
- When every score is final, an admin taps **Finalise session · award BADDEST PLAYER**. Each winner gets +5 points, once.
- If a correction changes the winner later, **Update the award** reverses the old +5 and gives the new one. Nothing is ever given twice.
- The +5 reaches the BAD APP – Master sheet at the next points sync, as an Adjustment row with the reason "BADDEST PLAYER".
